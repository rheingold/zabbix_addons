## Visual Distinction Strategy

Colors are ordered to maximize contrast between consecutive categories:
1. **CPU** (Navy Blue) - Cool, dark
2. **CPU2** (Dark Gray/Brown) - Neutral, very dark
3. **RAM** (Pastel Blue/Cyan) - Cool, light
4. **Storage/DB** (Orange/Brown) - Warm, medium
5. **Storage/DB2** (Yellow) - Warm, bright
6. **StorageHW** (Brown/Maroon) - Warm, dark
7. **Network** (Cyan/Turquoise) - Cool, bright
8. **Network2** (Purple/Violet) - Cool-warm, medium
9. **Services** (Pea Green) - Warm, medium
10. **Services2** (Dark Green) - Cool, dark
11. **Services3** (Neon Green) - Cool, very bright
12. **Services4** (Dark Green/Cyan) - Cool, very dark
13. **Services5** (Neon Cyan/Yellow-Green) - Cool-warm, very bright

Each category alternates between base color intensities to ensure visual separation when multiple series are displayed together.
